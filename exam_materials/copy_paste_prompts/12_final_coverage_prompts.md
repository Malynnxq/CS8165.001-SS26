## F-01 - Complete Exam Readiness Map

```text
Course: CS8165.001 Interactive Computer Graphics.
Full topic coverage:
- Lecture 01 Introduction: Computer graphics generates images from descriptions; interactive graphics adds a strict time budget and a feedback loop with the user. Terms: raster image, pixel, color depth, 3D model, primitive, interactive graphics.
- Lecture 02 Rendering Pipeline: Rendering is a sequence of representation changes: models become vertices, primitives, fragments, tested fragments, and finally framebuffer updates. Terms: application stage, geometry stage, rasterization, fragment, framebuffer, double buffering.
- Lecture 03 Geometric Transformations: Transformations change how points, vectors, normals, and coordinate frames are expressed across model, world, view, and clip-related spaces. Terms: homogeneous coordinates, translation, rotation, scaling, matrix composition, normal transformation.
- Lecture 04 Geometric Projection: Projection maps camera-space geometry into clip coordinates and then normalized device coordinates before viewport mapping. Terms: view volume, orthographic projection, perspective projection, clip coordinates, perspective divide, viewport transformation.
- Lecture 05 Clipping: Clipping classifies geometry against boundaries and keeps, rejects, or cuts primitives before rasterization. Terms: clipping, Cohen-Sutherland, Sutherland-Hodgman, Cyrus-Beck, outcode, intersection point.
- Lecture 06 Rasterization: Rasterization converts continuous projected geometry into discrete fragment candidates and interpolated per-fragment attributes. Terms: scan conversion, triangle coverage, barycentric coordinates, interpolation, aliasing, fragment candidate.
- Lecture 07 Visibility Determination: Visibility algorithms decide which candidate surface is actually seen from the current viewpoint. Terms: depth buffer, z-buffer algorithm, Painter's algorithm, BSP tree, Warnock algorithm, ray casting.
- Lecture 08 Local Illumination: Local illumination computes color from surface orientation, light direction, view direction, and material response. Terms: surface normal, ambient term, diffuse reflection, specular reflection, Phong model, material coefficient.
- Lecture 09 Texturing: Texturing uses coordinates and sampler state to fetch stored data and interpret it in a shader. Terms: texture, texel, UV coordinates, filtering, mipmap, environment mapping.
- Lecture 10 Shadows: Shadows are visibility tests from the light source, not only dark shapes seen by the camera. Terms: shadow, occluder, receiver, shadow map, shadow volume, projective shadow.
```
