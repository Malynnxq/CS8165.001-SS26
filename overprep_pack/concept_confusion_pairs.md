Concept Confusion Pairs
=======================
For each row: choose left/right/both/neither, then explain with one sentence.

Pair	Decision Rule
Fragment vs Pixel	A fragment is a candidate produced before final tests; a pixel is final image storage/output.
Clipping vs Culling	Clipping cuts primitives against a region; culling rejects whole primitives/objects based on tests.
View transform vs Projection transform	View transform moves world into camera space; projection maps camera space into clip/projected space.
Texture vs Texel	A texture is the sampled data field; a texel is one sample in that field.
Diffuse vs Specular	Diffuse is broad view-independent reflection; specular is view-dependent highlight.
Z-buffer vs Shadow map	Z-buffer stores camera-view depth; shadow map stores light-view depth.
Shadow acne vs Peter panning	Acne is self-shadowing artifacts; peter panning is detached shadows from too much bias.
Rasterization vs Fragment shading	Rasterization creates fragments; fragment shading computes fragment outputs.
Orthographic projection vs Perspective projection	Orthographic lacks perspective shortening; perspective uses homogeneous divide.
Object coordinates vs World coordinates	Object coordinates are local model coordinates; world coordinates place objects in the scene.
