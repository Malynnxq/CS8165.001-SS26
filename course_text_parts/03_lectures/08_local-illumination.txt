Reflection Modeling 1/2
 All light sources can be described by their Spectral Density Functions
(cid:127) Natural light: sun, fire, ...
(cid:127) Artificial light: light bulb, laser, neon tube, LED, ...
 All material properties can be described by Spectral Response Functions
(cid:127) Glass, Water: SRF for transmission
(cid:127) Surfaces: SRF for reflection
 Sensors (camera pixels, film, eye) can be described as SRF
 Definition of visible color = integral over (result SDF x sensor SRF)
