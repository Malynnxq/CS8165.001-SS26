 Name derived from Word for Picture Element
 Pixel is a sample with associated sample values (=pixel components)
(cid:127) Color
(cid:127) Transparency (=alpha value)
(cid:127) Depth (i.e., distance from viewer (=z value))
(cid:127) …
 Each sample value stored at given precision
(bit depth)
(cid:127) e.g. 24 Bit color, 8 Bit alpha + 24 Bit depth

Chapter Summary
 Raster images are formed of pixels to which colors, transparencies and depth
are assigned
 In interactive computer graphics 3D models are mostly approximated by
polygonal models
 Most frequent building blocks of polygonal models are triangles,
which are formed of vertices and edges
 Rendering translates 3D models into raster images
